# Scene Composition Rules

## Composition Goal
- Keep corridor as calm spatial spine.
- Keep artworks as focal punctuation points.
- Maintain visual rhythm through repeated structural modules.

## Spatial Proportions
- Default corridor ratio is intentionally wide enough for cinematic reading.
- Preserve believable human-scale camera height and corridor dimensions.
- Avoid sudden proportion shifts between scenes unless explicitly authored.

## Artwork Spacing
- Use config-driven spacing (`corridor.artworkSpacing`) as rhythm control.
- Spacing should allow:
  - anticipation during travel
  - clear focus hold
  - decompression before next reveal

## Focal Point Rules
- Each artwork moment needs:
  - clear camera approach
  - stable look target
  - readable artwork + side-text composition (if enabled)
- Do not stack competing focal lights in focus windows.

## Symmetry / Asymmetry Balance
- Corridor structure can remain mostly symmetrical.
- Artwork side alternation provides controlled asymmetry.
- Side-text placement can bias composition intentionally but must remain balanced over sequence.

## Negative Space Usage
- Preserve breathing room around artworks.
- Do not fill all wall/floor surfaces with decorative elements.
- Empty corridor segments are pacing assets, not unused space.

## Visual Rhythm
- Alternate tension and calm through:
  - light intensity changes
  - spacing cadence
  - approach/hold/return timing
- Avoid repetitive monotony by subtle, rule-based variation.

## Color / Contrast Composition
- Background/fog/wall values should support artwork prominence.
- Contrast must support depth and silhouette, not flatten scene.
- Avoid over-saturated accents that overpower artwork surfaces.
