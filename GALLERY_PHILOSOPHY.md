# Scrollix Gallery3D Philosophy

## Experience Intent
- Curated cinematic exploration, not gameplay.
- Spatial continuity over spectacle.
- Slow discovery over rapid stimulation.

## Emotional Goals
- Immersion: user feels inside a coherent place.
- Curiosity: each artwork reveal invites forward motion.
- Calm: motion and transitions reduce cognitive noise.
- Tension-release: contrast moments without aggression.

## Artistic Direction
- Liminal corridor language: repetition, depth, controlled emptiness.
- Minimal geometry, expressive light, restrained palette shifts.
- Artworks are narrative anchors; environment is atmospheric frame.
- Negative space is intentional and should remain readable.

## Pacing Philosophy
- Movement cadence: intro drift -> focus approach -> hold -> return.
- Camera must breathe; avoid abrupt acceleration/deceleration.
- Temporal emphasis should land on artwork focus and atmospheric transitions.

## Cinematic Principles
- Composition-first camera behavior.
- Light as emotional cue, not just illumination.
- Consistent visual rhythm between corridor modules.
- Reversible journey quality (forward/backward scroll symmetry).

## Interaction Philosophy
- Scroll translates to weighted progression, never twitch movement.
- Input should feel inertial, damped, and physically plausible.
- Transitions should be legible under mouse wheel and trackpad variability.

## Should Feel Like
- immersive
- elegant
- premium
- physical
- restrained
- atmospheric

## Must Never Feel Like
- arcade-like
- hyperactive
- snappy-gamey
- chaotic
- over-designed UI demo
- effect-first tech showcase
